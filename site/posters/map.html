How the pieces fit

  1. A room is drawn: in the medium's rooms, or in the room builder.
  2. wmdcfg compiles it into the RF of every radio pair.
  3. wmediumd delivers each frame between the hwsim radios.
  4. The agents and a hundred clients, in containers, associate.
  5. The controller (RDK or prplMesh) measures them.
  6. The optimizer decides and steers through the controller.
  7. The live room shows it; the room suite checks it.
  8. EMOSA brings unchanged OpenSync pods into step 4, as EasyMesh agents.
